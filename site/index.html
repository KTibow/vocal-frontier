The board

Across: speed

S = √(CPU × GPU)

Times faster than HTDemucs, end to end, on a full clip including the STFT. Half is CPU, half is GPU, each a geometric mean of its devices.

Native and browser run on a Ryzen 5 3501U mini PC, Vega 8 is its GPU, Adreno X1 a Snapdragon X Elite laptop, Mali Valhall a Nothing phone, and Gen12LP an Intel ChromeOS laptop.

Fail on a device, or run slower than 0.05× there, and that device scores 0.05×. Failing one GPU takes a model that's 4× everywhere else down to 2.31×. Failing the browser takes it down to 1.34×.

Up: quality

Q = mean SDR, dB

Mean of three sets of 50 clips of 30 s: musdb30 and cc30, both public and rebuildable, and private30, which only the maintainers can run. An entry more than 1 dB below HTDemucs (under 10.0 dB) earns nothing.

Each device's output also has to match the native output on a public test clip, within 0.5 dB. Fast and wrong counts as a failure.

The money: area

$ ∝ new area

One unit is one doubling of speed times one dB. Your points earn the area that nobody before you covers, in order of submission. Anything not more than 5% faster or 0.1 dB better than an earlier entry counts as a copy and earns nothing. The chart starts at 1/16× HTDemucs, so speed under that earns nothing.

The pot divides over all new area, including what the maintainers' own models added. That share is never paid out, and it shrinks the more you add.
